About Us

South Central Mindanao Mission (SCMM) is a local field of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, organized in 2024 to serve believers and communities across Cotabato City, Kidapawan City, Tacurong City, and the provinces of Maguindanao, North Cotabato, and Sultan Kudarat. SCMM operates under the Southeastern Philippine Union Mission, within the worldwide structure of the Adventist Church.

Our Mission
To make disciples of Jesus Christ who live as His loving witnesses, sharing the everlasting gospel with all people in preparation for His soon return.

Guided by the Bible and the Holy Spirit, SCMM pursues this mission through Christ-like living, teaching, healing, and serving — reaching communities across our territory through local churches, ministries, and outreach programs.
