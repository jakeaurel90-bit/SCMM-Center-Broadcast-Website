Organization

South Central Mindanao Mission (SCMM) is one administrative level within the worldwide structure of the Seventh-day Adventist Church. Like every Adventist mission field, SCMM operates within a four-tier system of church governance that connects local congregations all the way up to the world church headquarters.

General Conference of Seventh-day Adventists
The worldwide headquarters of the Church, based in Silver Spring, Maryland, USA. It oversees the entire global Adventist movement.
Southern Asia-Pacific Division
A regional arm of the General Conference overseeing Adventist work across the Philippines and neighboring countries in the Asia-Pacific region.
Southeastern Philippine Union Mission (SEPUM)
The union-level body overseeing Adventist missions across Southeastern Mindanao, including Davao and the surrounding provinces.
South Central Mindanao Mission (SCMM)
Our local mission field, serving Cotabato City, Kidapawan City, Tacurong City, and the provinces of Maguindanao, North Cotabato, and Sultan Kudarat.
Local Churches and Congregations
The individual Seventh-day Adventist churches and companies throughout SCMM's territory, where members worship and serve their communities directly.

SCMM was organized in 2024 from territory formerly part of the Southern Mindanao Mission. As of mid-2025, SCMM serves a territory of over 5 million people through 185 local churches with a combined membership of over 83,000.
